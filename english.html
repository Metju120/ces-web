Czech Endodontic Society

Education, collaboration and endodontics · Founded in 2007

The Czech Endodontic Society (Česká endodontická společnost, ČES) supports education, clinical knowledge and collaboration in endodontics. Its goals include modern treatment techniques and continuing professional education. ČES is a member of ESE and IFEA.
