OR WHEN ORTHOGRADE TREATMENT IS NOT AN OPTION

Prof. Dr. Paula Villa

Despite the high success rate of root canal treatments, some failing cases require resolution

through retrograde surgical endodontics. Over the past two decades, this field has undergone a

dramatic transformation, with new techniques and approaches constantly emerging—pushing

the boundaries of what was once considered impossible.

Join Professor Paula Villa—the queen of retrograde surgery—for an in-depth exploration of

both fundamental principles and advanced techniques in microsurgical endodontics.

During the theoretical lecture, you will become familiar with the indications, contraindications, and limitations of microsurgical endodontics. You will learn how to simplify surgical procedures through proper case assessment, thorough planning, and small practical tips that reduce the complexity of the surgery itself. Last but not least, you will learn to recognize the key factors that influence the success of microsurgical endodontic treatment.

June 28–29, 2025 – PRACTICAL COURSE

(sold out, limited number of participants)

First Faculty of Medicine, Charles University, Kateřinská 1660/32, Prague 2

In this 2-day hands-on course, you will practice every step of microsurgical endodontics.

From accurate CBCT interpretation and treatment planning, to hard tissue management

(accessing the bony crypt, root-end resection, and retrograde preparation) using various

techniques—rotary or piezo—through to soft tissue management including incision design

and suturing. Each participant will have their own operating microscope and anatomical

model to perform all phases of microsurgical endodontic procedures on phantom models.
